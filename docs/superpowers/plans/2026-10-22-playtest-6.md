# Playtest 6 Feedback Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:**
- Every round lasts 20 s: 2 steps per round (+1 from the own base) and 1 camp per round for the whole game.
- Wolves and Raptors are 1 step apart.
- A gank that kills nobody costs more HP and pushes the gankers' wave.
- The player chooses where their jungler starts.
- An objective continued 2 rounds in a row, each time with the numbers, is taken.
- Voidgrubs get a named timer on the map and a log line when taken.
- Meeting an enemy in a contact reveals its stats.
- A broadcast-style score bar across the top, with each team's towers taken.

**Architecture:** unchanged (`web → engine → data`, contracts in `src/contracts`). **Branch:** `feat/mvp-and-playtest` (the user wants every playtest on this one branch; task worktrees merge back into it).

**Spec:** `README.md`. Each task updates the sections it changes.

## Context and decisions (playtest 5 → 6)

Status: approved by the user on 2026-10-22 (with 50 % HP for a failed gank); executed subagent-driven.

This plan comes from the playtest of `feat/mvp-and-playtest` at `a3f833c` (playtest 5 plus the movement additions of 2026-10-22: new links, +1 step from the base, contact = vision).

**Playtest feedback** (the user's words, condensed):
- Path fixes: Wolves and Raptors are 1 move apart. (A second path bullet was left empty: nothing to do until the user fills it in.)
- A gank with no kill must still deal more damage and push the wave to the next stage.
- We must be able to choose where our jungler starts.
- Continuing an objective 2 times in a row, each time outnumbering the enemy, completes it.
- Don't forget timers on the voidgrubs ("larves") and the log once they are killed.
- Crossing a champion (in the jungle or in lane) shows its stats.
- The number of towers taken by each team is missing.
- Add the same UI as competitive matches along the top, to get all the information.
- All phases should have the same round length: **20 s everywhere** (user's answer).
- Dropped by the user: "phase 2: the jungler does 2 camps, phase 3: 3 camps". With 20 s rounds everywhere it stays 1 camp per round, and the movement range stays the same all game.

**Decisions** (user's unless marked *(call)*):
1. **Round length.** Every phase has `roundSeconds: 20`. The game still runs from 1:30 to 25:10: 71 rounds instead of 37. The step budget is 2 everywhere (+1 from the own base).
2. **What keeps its per-round value** *(call)*: these were already tuned for the 20 s rounds of the early game, so they stay as they are:
   - wards (3 per round, 2 rounds), unwards;
   - regeneration, trade and fight HP losses, trade lead;
   - tower damage: `towers.pushDamage` is indexed by the number of attackers on the tower, not by phase, and `lanes.pushBonus` / `towers.absentLanerChip` are per round too. So late-game towers fall faster in minutes than today, at the early game's per-round pace. The benchmark decides whether AI data needs re-tuning;
   - 1 camp per round;
   - objective capture (2 rounds);
   - AI windows counted in rounds (`knownRounds`, "soon", conversion).
3. **What keeps its real duration** *(call)*: values that only made sense with 40 s and 60 s rounds are rescaled so the game's pace in minutes stays the same:
   - **Death timer**: today 1 round (+1 from stage 4), so 20 s, 20 s, 40 s, 60 s, 120 s. New data `death.roundsByStage` [1, 1, 2, 3, 6] replaces `lateExtraRounds`/`lateExtraRoundsFromStage`.
   - **Baron buff**: `buffs.baronRounds` 3 (180 s at 60 s rounds) → 9.
   - Recall stays 1 round (20 s).
   - Then re-tune the idle benchmark with data only, and record old → new.
4. **Wolves ↔ Raptors**: 1 step, mirrored on the red side. This is a jungle-to-jungle crossing over the mid lane: not a lane change.
5. **Gank with no kill**:
   - The ganked champions lose **50 % of their max HP** (user's number): `fight.gankHpLoss` 15 → 50, at `health.max` 100. HP alone never kills.
   - The wave push (user's rule). It applies when a gank contact ends with no kill and either the gankers won the fight or the ganked side fled:
     - if the lane's wave is not yet at the ganked side's tower, it goes straight there;
     - if it already is, the gankers take a plate: 1 damage on that lane's front tower, with plate gold before `platesGoldUntil`, like any plate. After 14:00 there are no plate rewards, but the damage still applies *(call)*.
   - This replaces the lane's normal wave move for that round. Once per lane and round.
6. **Jungler start**:
   - The start screen gets a "Départ du jungler" choice: any of the 6 own camps.
   - The default is Blue Buff or Red Buff picked by the seed, as today.
   - The chosen camp starts cleared, like the buff today. A small camp gives its own small-camp gold, XP and respawn (`smallRespawn`), not the buff's *(call)*.
   - The enemy's round-1 sighting of the start camp is unchanged.
   - The AI keeps the seeded buff.
7. **Objective with the numbers 2 rounds in a row**:
   - Today any enemy in the river resets the capture.
   - New: on a pit, the side with **more acting champions on the pit than the enemy** progresses the capture, even when contested, provided it chose "Continuer l'objectif" (an uncontested side progresses as today).
   - Its count is taken after the contact resolves (flees and retreats applied, deaths removed). The enemy count is every alive enemy still on the pit, acting or not *(call)*. The Scuttle keeps today's rule (any enemy in the river contests it).
   - If the same side does so 2 rounds in a row, the objective is taken at the end of the 2nd round.
   - Equal numbers: no progress, and the capture resets.
   - Baron keeps `minChampions` 3. `instantAt` (4+ champions) still takes it in one round *(call: unchanged)*.
8. **Voidgrubs timer and log**:
   - The Baron pit's countdown on the map names what it counts down to: "Larves 6:00", then "Héraut 14:00", then "Nashor 20:00".
   - While the grubs are up it also shows when they despawn (14:00).
   - The log line uses the article: "Rouge prend les Larves", like "le Dragon".
9. **Meeting reveals stats**:
   - Every champion in a contact (lane or jungle, prompted or automatic) is "met" by the other side that round.
   - Until it is seen again, its enemy card shows the level, HP and power it had then, labelled "Croisé : <position> (round N)".
   - Seen again, the live values replace it. Fog of war otherwise holds.
10. **Broadcast score bar** *(call on layout)*: a bar across the top like a competitive match broadcast.
    - Blue team on the left, red on the right, the game clock and round in the middle.
    - Per team: kills, total gold earned (with the gold difference), towers destroyed, dragons, voidgrubs, heralds, barons, plates.
    - It replaces the current `Scoreboard` line. All of it is public information, as in a broadcast.
    - The team panels keep their per-champion cards.
