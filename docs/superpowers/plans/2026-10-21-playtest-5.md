# Playtest 5 Feedback Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:**
- Movement becomes **range + action**: a step budget set by the round's length, where an action costs one step. It runs on a corrected map graph.
- A prompted fight-or-flee whenever enemies share a lane, the river or a camp, outside standard laning.
- The river (Scuttle + pit) is one grouped fight.
- HP losses are visible.
- The AI never stands on a tower without its wave.
- Wards last 2 rounds and report enemies passing by.
- Recall is shown on the map and in the replay.
- A "Faire le Dragon / Nashor" prompt.

**Architecture:** unchanged (`web → engine → data`, contracts in `src/contracts`). Branch `feat/playtest-5`, on top of `feat/playtest-4`.

**Spec:** `README.md`. Each task updates the sections it changes:
- Task 1: "Movement".
- Task 2: "Fights", "Neutral objectives".
- Task 3: "Vision", "Lead, gold and recall".
- Task 4: "AI opponent".
- Task 5: "Game screen".

The reference for reachability is the "Rift Move Graph" artifact, in its *Portée + action* mode. Its edge list is copied in Task 1.

## Context and decisions (playtest 4 → 5)

This plan comes from the playtest of `feat/playtest-4`. Status: written, not reviewed by the user yet, nothing implemented. Execution mode: subagent-driven (recommended, not yet confirmed).

**Playtest feedback** (the user's words, condensed):
- A jungler reaching an enemy camp in one move clears it like its own, and should see its respawn timer.
- Champions don't seem to lose HP on ganks or when outnumbered.
- Killing the Scuttle puts a ward on its spot, in addition to the 3 wards. Show the ward icon on the dead Scuttle too.
- Wards report enemies passing by, with a log line and in the replay. A ward lasts 2 rounds.
- The AI never goes under an enemy T2 while the T1 stands. It never stands on a tower without its minions: hard rule, no "stupid dive" mistake.
- In Silver, the AI wanders around for no reason.
- The step before a recall is not drawn. Add a recall icon to the replay: League's B-button icon.
- Drake and Nashor reachable from a lane, including mid, and pit ↔ T1 directly. A "Faire le Dragon / Nashor" prompt when on the pit.
- The Scuttle and its neighbouring pit are one grouped fight:
  - doing the Dragon or Nashor with the Scuttle up takes both in the same round;
  - when contested, each side chooses flee, fight or continue the objective;
  - doing only the Scuttle never triggers the Dragon or Nashor choice.
- Jungle log: "se déplace et réalise le camp".
- **Playtest situation.** An enemy Fiora on my T1 while my Darius and my jungler are on the lane. Fiora must not be able to move under my T1 without her wave: she must face the gank.
- Whenever enemies share a jungle point or a lane, prompt fight or flee, except in standard laning.
- **Movement model** (user's proposal):
  - a step budget per round, which depends on the round's length;
  - an action costs a step;
  - you can arrive somewhere without being able to act that round;
  - two points can be in range with one before the other.
- Keep actions on the mid lane middle for now: it reaches both pits and both Scuttles.

**Diagnosis** (code on `feat/playtest-4`):
- **River and enemy camps.** Every Scuttle and pit is linked to all 12 camps, so a 2-move jungler reaches 25–29 of the 39 positions. `junglerObjectiveReach` is a no-op.
- **Enemy camp timers.** Clearing an enemy camp works, but `viewFor` drops enemy camp timers.
- **HP.**
  - Pressure (2v1 without a gank) never costs HP.
  - A gank where both sides flee, or a tied gank, is free.
  - A loss of 15 is netted to −7 by same-round regen.
  - No log line shows HP.
- **AI.** `pointless` (priority 100, Silver 0.4) picks any reachable position, enemy T2s included. `conversion` and `herald` ignore the wave.
- **Wards and Scuttle.** The ward lifetime is hard-coded to 1 round. The Scuttle ward is put on the pit, not on the Scuttle.
- **Recall.** `MapView` skips arrows for recall orders, and `buildReplay` has no `recall` case. There is no recall asset.
- **Objectives.** A pit objective is taken by staying, with no order. Contacts are grouped per position.

**Reference:** the "Rift Move Graph" page, https://claude.ai/artifact/3kbcfFeutbMYnjLh4vtHyn (private). It shows the current graph, the 1-move proposal and the *Portée + action* mode, which has a 20 s / 40 s / 1 min budget selector. At budget 4, a position acts on 23 of 38 positions on average. If 1-minute rounds feel too mobile, cap the budget at 3 in `budgetBySeconds`.

## Review Focus

- **The playtest situation.** An enemy laner stands alone on my T1. My laner and my jungler are in the same lane, on another position. This must give a prompted contact, never a silent pass-through *(Task 2)*.
- **Arrival without action never acts.** A champion arriving on its last budget step never clears a camp, takes the Scuttle, starts or continues an objective, or farms its wave. It still takes part in contacts *(Tasks 1, 2)*.
- **Mirror symmetry.** Every movement and river rule holds for red exactly as for blue *(Task 1)*.

- **Scuttle ward.** It never consumes one of the 3 wards per round, and never replaces a ward placed by an order *(Task 3)*.

**Interfaces (produced):**
- `stepBudget(rules: Rules, round: number): number` reads the round length from `roundWindow` and looks it up in `rules.moves.budgetBySeconds` = `{ "20": 2, "40": 3, "60": 4 }`.
- `reachableFrom(data, position, budget, side): Record<string, number>` gives the distance to every position within `budget`.
- `canAct(distance: number, budget: number): boolean` is `distance < budget`. Staying (distance 0) can act.
- `route(data, from, to, side): string[]` is the deterministic shortest path, both ends included. Ties: lane position, then camp, then river, then id (today's `bestVia` rank).
- The `moved` event gains `acted: boolean`.

  - Changing lane still ends the move.
- [ ] **Actions.** A champion acts this round when `canAct(distance(previous, position), budget)`. Only an acting champion:

  - takes a Scuttle, or starts or continues a pit objective (`captureObjectives`, through a set of acting champion ids);

### Task 2 (engine): zone contacts, grouped river, visible HP

**Files:**
- Engine: `packages/engine/src/contacts.ts`, `packages/engine/src/contracts/contacts.ts`, `packages/engine/src/contracts/events.ts`, `packages/engine/src/round.ts`, `packages/engine/src/objectives.ts`, `packages/engine/src/vision.ts` (`eventsFor`).
- Data: `packages/data/data/rules.json` and its contract.
- Tests: `packages/engine/test/contacts.test.ts`, `lane-contacts.test.ts`, `health.test.ts`, `objectives.test.ts`, `round.test.ts`.

**Interfaces (produced, used by Tasks 4 and 5):**
- `Contact.id` becomes a zone id: `lane-top` / `lane-mid` / `lane-bot`, `river-top` / `river-bot`, or the position id for a camp.
- New field `Contact.positions: string[]`: the zone positions where involved champions arrived, sorted. `Contact.position` is where the fight resolves.
- `Choice` gains `'objective'` (keep taking the objective).
- New event `{ type: 'hpLost'; champion: string; amount: number }`.

- [ ] **Zones.**
  - A lane zone is the towers and the middle of one lane.
  - `river-top` is `scuttle-top` + `baron-pit`, and `river-bot` is `scuttle-bot` + `dragon-pit`.
  - Each camp is its own zone. Bases are never contacts.
  - `detectContacts` groups arrivals by zone.
- [ ] **Standard laning.** A lane zone is standard laning when both sides have the same number of champions there, all home laners of that lane. It keeps the automatic `trade` contact from stances.
- [ ] **Every other zone with both sides present is prompted.**
  - Kinds: `gank` (existing definition); `pressure` (uneven lane, nobody from elsewhere); `objective` (river); `invade` (camp); `skirmish` (anything else in a lane).
  - Defaults are unchanged: the smaller side flees, otherwise it fights.
  - In a lane zone, a smaller side whose champions all stand on their own standing tower also gets `farm`:
    - it stays and farms at `economy.pressureFarmShare`;
    - it loses `fight.pressureHpLoss` (new, 10) when the bigger side fights;
    - it dies only if the bigger side fights and wins by more than `aiTuning.allInMargin`.
- [ ] **Where the fight happens** (`Contact.position`):
  1. The position of the smaller side. If its champions are on several positions, the one with most of them.
  2. On even numbers, the zone position with the most champions.
  3. Remaining ties go to the lowest id.

  Champions who don't flee are moved onto it. A tower there counts for its defenders.
- [ ] **Grouped river.**
  - An acting champion on a pit whose objective is up starts or continues it. When the pit objective is taken, the river's Scuttle is taken in the same round if it is up. It is shared like the objective's reward.
  - An acting champion on the Scuttle takes only the Scuttle. It never starts the pit objective.
  - A contested river (`objective` contact) offers each side `fight`, `flee` and `objective` (only to a side with an acting champion on the pit or the Scuttle). *(design calls)*
    - **`objective` against `flee`:** the objective (and the Scuttle) proceeds.
    - **`objective` against `fight`:** it is a fight. The `objective` side's score is multiplied by `fight.objectiveFocus` (new, 0.8). If it still wins, it takes the objective this round, as well as the kills.
    - **`objective` against `objective`:** a race. The higher fight score takes it, and nobody dies or loses HP. On a tie, nobody takes it.
- [ ] **HP made visible.**
  - Every HP loss emits `hpLost`.
  - A side that flees a side committing to fight while outnumbered loses `fight.gankHpLoss`. This extends today's gank-only rule to every prompted contact.
  - A champion who lost HP this round gets no `regenPerRound` at the end of it.
  - `eventsFor` shows `hpLost` for own champions, and for enemies visible after the round.
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - **Playtest situation:** red top alone on `top-t1-blue`, blue top and blue jungle on `top-lane`. This gives a prompted `gank` at `top-t1-blue`, and the blue tower counts for blue.
  - Standard 1v1 and 2v2 lanes stay automatic.
  - Lone ADC on its T1 against 2: it gets `farm` / `flee`. Against a fight it loses 10 HP and nothing more when the gap ≤ margin.
  - River contacts:
    - Blue on `scuttle-bot` and red on `dragon-pit` give one `river-bot` contact.
    - `objective` vs `flee` takes the Dragon (when due) and the Scuttle.
    - `objective` vs `fight` applies 0.8.
    - `objective` vs `objective` gives the race result, with no deaths.
  - Uncontested river:
    - Taking the Dragon with the Scuttle up gives both in one round.
    - Standing on the Scuttle alone takes only the Scuttle, with no `objectiveStarted`.
  - Arrival-only:
    - An arrival-only champion on the pit cannot choose `objective`.
    - It still fights.
  - HP: a trade loser at 100 ends the round at 80, with no regen. `hpLost` events are filtered by fog.
- [ ] README "Fights" and "Neutral objectives". Run all checks. Commit.

### Task 4 (engine AI): range-aware targets, towers, wandering, contacts

**Files:** `packages/engine/src/ai/heuristics.ts`, `packages/engine/src/ai/index.ts`, `packages/engine/src/ai/simulate.ts`, `packages/data/data/ai-profiles.json`, `packages/data/data/ai-tuning.json`, tests `packages/engine/test/ai.test.ts`, `benchmark.test.ts`.

Depends on Tasks 1 and 2.

- [ ] **Range-aware targets.**
  - A target that needs an action (a camp, a Scuttle, a pit objective, a laner's farm) is reached only where the AI can act. Otherwise the AI walks toward it, and its candidate scores reflect the lost round.
  - `step()` uses `stepBudget` and `canAct`.
- [ ] **Tower rule** (hard, never a mistake).
  - An AI champion may end on an enemy tower position only when it is that lane's front tower and the AI's wave is at it (`ourWave(view, lane) === 1`).
  - One predicate, `towerAllowed(ctx, position): boolean`, filters `step()`, `pointless`, `conversion` and `herald`. With nothing allowed, the champion stays put.
- [ ] **Wandering.**
  - `pointless` picks only among the AI's own side camps, the river and its own lane positions.
  - Halve `pointlessPlay` for Iron, Bronze and Silver (Silver 0.4 → 0.2), and record old → new.
- [ ] **Contacts.** `contactChoice` / `aiContactChoice` handle the new kinds and the `farm` and `objective` options:
  - the AI farms under its tower when outnumbered without a gank;
  - it flees a gank it would lose;
  - it chooses `objective` when its score × `objectiveFocus` still wins.
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - **Tower fuzz:** 30 seeds per rank, every AI order checked against `towerAllowed`. No rank and no forced mistake orders an enemy tower without the wave.
  - **Herald** waits for the wave.
  - **Pointless:** a forced `pointlessPlay` stays in its allowed pool.
  - **Arrival-only camps:** an AI jungler 2 steps from a camp at budget 2 does not target it as a clear.
  - **Contact choices:**
    - `farm` on a `pressure` contact when on its tower;
    - `objective` when it still wins.
- [ ] Run the benchmark. Re-tune data only if it fails, and record old → new. README "AI opponent". Commit.
