**Decisions** (user's unless marked *(call)*):
1. **Timeline**:
   - Round 0 covers the start of the game up to 0:55; round 1 starts at 0:55.
   - Rounds stay 20 s. The game ends at **25:15** *(call: 25:10 does not fall on the 0:55 + 20 s grid)*, which gives 73 rounds after round 0.
   - The phase boundaries move onto the grid *(call)*: 0:55 – 2:55 – 6:15 – 14:15 – 20:15 – 25:15. The 2:55 boundary matches the Scuttle spawn, and the phase names stay.
   - The verdict time moves to 25:15.
2. **Timers** (from the table):
   - Camps get a first spawn per camp type: buffs, Wolves and Raptors at 55 s; Krugs and Gromp at 67 s. Krugs and Gromp spawn inside round 1, so they count as up by that round's end, like objectives.
   - Respawns unchanged.
   - Scuttles at 175 s; voidgrubs at 480 s, despawn 885 s; Herald at 900 s, despawn 1185 s.
   - Dragon and Baron unchanged.
   - Every derived text follows: map timers, round summary, phase names, teaching, README.
3. **Round 0 placement**:
   - Before round 1, a placement round. Each side puts each of its 5 champions on any position of its own side (own towers, own camps, own base) or on a lane middle (top, mid or bot "voie").
   - River positions (Scuttles, pits) are not allowed *(call: they belong to neither side)*.
   - **Starting positions** *(call)*: several champions may share a position. Defaults are today's starting positions: laners on their own T1, the jungler on the seeded buff.
   - **The UI**: round 0 uses the usual order screen with every allowed position reachable (no step budget), and "Valider le placement".
   - **Nothing resolves in round 0**: no contact, no farm, no camp (none is up).
   - **Vision**: after round 0 the usual vision applies. At round 1 each side sees enemies on its lanes or positions as usual, and the enemy jungler's start is visible in round 1 as today.
   - **The AI** places its champions with a simple rule *(call)*: laners on their own T1; the jungler on a buff (seeded, as today).
   - **"Départ du jungler" removed** *(call)*: the start-screen choice and the autostart `jungle=` param are replaced by round 0. Placing the jungler on a camp there pre-clears nothing: camps spawn at 0:55, and the jungler clears its first camp in round 1 like any other.
5. **Quit the game** *(call on the details)*: a "Quitter la partie" button in the score bar, available on every game screen. It asks for confirmation inside the page ("Quitter la partie ? Oui / Non"), never a browser dialog, then goes back to the start screen (`store.reset`).
4. **No lane-to-lane join**: a champion whose arrival position is in another lane than a lane contact cannot join it. Example: mid T1 is 1 step from top T1, but a mid laner on its T1 cannot join a top lane fight. Joining a lane fight from the jungle or the river stays allowed.
