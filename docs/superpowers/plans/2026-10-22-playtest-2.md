# Playtest 2 Feedback Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Apply the second round of playtest feedback:
- **Map links:** laners can reach the Scuttle from their lane, and the river connects to every jungle camp.
- **Jungler start:** the jungler begins the game with its first buff already done.
- **XP:** levels progress at a realistic pace.
- **Lanes:**
  - a 2v1 caused by a roam is lane pressure, not a gank;
  - each laner has a persistent stance instead of a prompt every round;
  - a laner with no order follows its wave;
  - lane pressure is computed automatically.
- **AI:** respects the laning phase.
- **Screen:**
  - teams in side columns, as in League;
  - a scoreboard with kills, towers, Dragons, Barons and the gold advantage;
  - a strength bar above each pawn;
  - the contact buttons are fixed.

**Architecture:** unchanged, `web → engine → data`, with contracts in `src/contracts`. **Spec:** `README.md`. The changed sections are "Core rules (Starting state)", "Fights" (ganks, lane pressure, lane stance, laners follow their wave, lane pressure computed), "Movement" (lane → Scuttle, river → jungle), "AI opponent" (laning phase) and "Game screen".

- The UI is in French. A user dev server may be running on port 3000: never stop it, and use other ports.

- A laner whose stance is Farm can never die in an even lane contact unless the other side is All-in *(Task 2)*.
- The auto-follow move must be a valid order: reachable, and never into the enemy base *(Task 2)*.
- Scoreboard kills must equal the death events. The gold advantage must equal the difference of `goldEarned` *(Task 1, Task 4)*.

### Task 1: Map links, jungler start, XP pace, scoreboard data

**Files:** `packages/data/data/map.json`, `packages/data/data/rules.json` (economy), `packages/engine/src/setup.ts`, `packages/engine/src/contracts/state.ts` and `view.ts`, `packages/engine/src/vision.ts` (viewFor), `packages/engine/src/economy.ts` (kills counter), plus tests.

- [ ] **Edges.**
  - Lane → Scuttle in 1 move: `top-t1-blue`, `top-lane` and `top-t1-red` link to `scuttle-top`. Bot mirrors this automatically through `buildGraph`. Mid already reaches both Scuttles.
  - River → jungle in 1 move: `scuttle-top`, `scuttle-bot`, `baron-pit` and `dragon-pit` each link to all 12 camps.
  - Tests: those distances are 1, the graph is still mirror-symmetric, and laner reach from lane T1s now includes the nearby Scuttle.
- [ ] **Jungler start.** In `newGame`, each jungler has already cleared its start buff camp:
  - the camp's `aliveAt` is `firstSpawn + buffRespawn`;
  - the jungler gets the buff gold and XP (`rules.camps.buffGold` and `buffXp`);
  - it stands on the camp;
  - the enemy's `lastSeen` of it stays as today.

  Tests for both junglers.
- [ ] **XP pace.**
  - Change `rules.economy` so that a solo laner who only farms reaches level 6 by about 6:30, level 11 by about 14:00 and level 16 by about 25:00. Starting point: `farmXpPerMin` 380 → 560, plus a new `duoXpShare` of 0.7, so each bot laner gets 70% of a solo laner's farm XP instead of the support getting `supportShare` of it.
  - Test: a solo laner who stays in lane is at level ≥ 6 at round 15, ≥ 11 at round 26, and ≥ 15 at the end.
- [ ] **Scoreboard data.**
  - Add `kills: Record<Side, number>` to `GameState`. `applyKill` increments it for the winners' side, once per victim.
  - `PlayerView` gains `scoreboard: { kills, towersDestroyed, dragons, barons, goldEarned: Record<Side, number> }`. `goldEarned` is the true team sum, the declared exception to the fog. `dragons` and `barons` come from `objectivesTaken`.
  - Tests: the kills counter matches the death events, and `scoreboard.goldEarned` matches the state.
- [ ] Run all checks, re-tune if the benchmark fails, then commit.

### Task 2: Lanes — gank vs lane pressure, stances, follow the wave, computed pressure

**Files:** `packages/engine/src/contracts/` (orders and state), `contacts.ts`, `round.ts`, `lanes.ts`, `movement.ts` (`defaultOrders`), `ai/heuristics.ts` (stances and contact choice), plus tests.

- [ ] **Gank detection.**
  - A lane contact is a `gank` only if the outnumbering side includes a champion whose `previous` position is **not** a position of that lane (it arrived this round).
  - Otherwise, an uneven lane contact is a new kind, `'pressure'`. It produces no prompt: options are `['farm']` for the smaller side, and the bigger side's stance applies.
  - Resolution: the wave is pushed by the bigger side and tower damage follows the existing rules. The smaller side dies only if the bigger side's stance is All-in **and** `resolveFight` says it wins by more than `aiTuning.allInMargin`. Otherwise nobody dies.
  - Tests: support roams away, so 2v1 gives a `pressure` contact with no deaths and a pushed wave. A jungler arrives, so it gives a `gank` as before.
- [ ] **Stances.**
  - `ChampionState.stance: 'farm' | 'trade' | 'allin'` (default `'farm'`).
  - A new optional order field `stance?` sets it persistently.
  - Even lane contacts (`trade` kind) resolve from the two stances with the existing farm/trade/fight rules (`allin` maps to `fight`), with no prompt. `detectContacts` marks them `auto: true`.
  - The AI sets its laners' stances from its margins (`allInMargin` and `tradeMargin`) instead of answering prompts.
  - Tests: farm vs farm gives no deaths, and farm vs allin resolves as a fight. The stance persists across rounds until it is changed.
- [ ] **Follow the wave.** In `defaultOrders` (and the AI's base order), a laner with no explicit order moves to its lane's "wave position":
  - wave −1 → the blue front tower;
  - wave 0 → the lane middle;
  - wave +1 → the red front tower;
  - this applies only if the position is reachable this round, else one step toward it.

  Tests cover a blue and a red laner for each wave value.
- [ ] **Computed pressure.**
  - Export `lanePressure(state, data, lane): Side | null`, the side whose laners' fight score (matchup + level + lead, from `resolveFight` scores, no tower) is higher, `null` on a tie.
  - Use it for the equal-count lane push (it replaces the I5 inline computation, same behaviour).
  - Expose `pressure: Record<Lane, Side | null>` in `PlayerView`. It is computed only from champions the viewer can see; if the enemy laner is not visible, use `null`.
  - Tests.
- [ ] Run all checks, re-tune if needed, then commit.
