# Playtest 7 Feedback Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:**
- Seen enemy recalls are shown.
- The mid T1 and T2 reach the own buffs in 1 step.
- 0 HP kills.
- Own champions without an order are flagged before validating.
- Fleeing always backs off toward the base.
- The objective is offered on the Dragon and the voidgrubs from the round they spawn.
- A recall follows the move and happens where the move ends.

## Context and decisions (playtest 6 → 7)

Status: written, awaiting the user's approval.

This plan comes from the playtest of `feat/mvp-and-playtest` at `5695b83`.

**Playtest feedback** (the user's words, condensed):
1. If we have vision on an enemy who recalls, show it too.
2. You can go from the mid to the blue buff in 1 move and back, and the same for the red buff. The user specified: from the mid T1 or T2, to the own side's blue or red buff only.
3. At 0 HP the champion is dead.
4. An indicator on a champion that does nothing, before validating the round.
5. Fleeing should logically move toward the base, not go back to the starting position. For example, a fight on the mid lane flees under the T1, or under the T2 if already under the T1.
6. I was on the Dragon and was not offered the objective; same for the voidgrubs.
7. The recall happens from the place reached after the move.
8. Add a rule: if a fight happens 1 move away from a champion, ask that champion whether it joins the fight. The enemy (AI) has the same option.
9. Show how many rounds are left to finish the objective, or a health bar.
10. Check that every enemy champion passing over a ward is really visible.
11. An enemy who dewards (clears one of our wards) is visible in the round it does it.

**Diagnosis** (code at `5695b83`):
- **Item 6 is a bug.**
  - Objectives spawn at the start of `resolveRound` (`round.ts:33`), so during the order phase of their spawn round the view still shows `objective: null`. The Dragon spawns in round 11 and the voidgrubs in round 14.
  - `objectivePromptEntries` (`web/src/components/map-marks.ts:138-148`) skips them. On resolve the capture starts on its own, and "Faire le Dragon" only appears the next round, half-way through.
  - The prompt is also never offered to a champion moving onto the pit; only to one already standing there.
  - Contacts already count "spawns by this round's end" (`contacts.ts:142`).
- **Item 7.** The engine recalls from the step position correctly (`round.ts:56-70`, `completeRecall`).
  - The UI drops a 2-step move silently when Recall is pressed, because a recall needs a step left. The champion then recalls from where it started, with no message.
  - Recall-then-move replaces the recall.
  - A recaller whose step lands in a contact that flees goes back to the start (`retreat`).
- **Item 1.** `eventsFor` keeps an enemy `recall` only if the enemy was visible or its position is in `seen`. `seen` excludes ward vision (`vision.ts:248-269`). The replay shows an enemy recall only if visible before or after.
- **Item 3.** HP losses go through `loseHp` → `changeHp`, which clamps at 0 and "never kills" (`round.ts:95-101`, `economy.ts:43`). Deaths only come from `fightVictims` → `applyKill`.
- **Item 4.** No indicator exists. `RoundPanel` wrongly says "reste en place" for laners who in fact follow their wave.
- **Item 5.** `retreat` (`round.ts:130-139`) sends a champion back to `previous`, and uses `retreatStep` only if it did not move. A champion coming from the enemy side flees forward.
- **Item 9.** The capture lives in `state.pits[id].capture = {side, startedRound}`. The UI shows no progress.

**Decisions** (user's unless marked *(call)*):
1. **Enemy recall seen**: an enemy recall is shown (log line and the recall icon in the replay) whenever the side sees the position where it recalls, ward vision included.
2. **Mid ↔ buffs**: the mid T1 and the mid T2 of a side are each 1 step from that side's Blue Buff and Red Buff. These are 4 new links per side, mirrored by the loader. Lane → camp is not a lane change.
3. **0 HP = dead**:
   - A champion whose HP reaches 0 from any loss dies like a fight victim (respawn timer, kill and gold for the other side).
   - The kill credit goes to the opponents in the contact that caused the loss *(call)*: for a trade, the other laners; for a flee or a gank, the side that stayed. They split it like fight winners.
   - Deaths from 0 HP count in the kills and appear in the log as kills.
4. **No-order indicator** *(call on what counts)*:
   - An own alive champion is "without order" when it has no pending order and its default is to stay where it is. A laner following its wave is not flagged, nor is a respawning champion (it has a default spawn).
   - Each one gets a marker on its map token and on its team card, and the round summary lists them ("Sans ordre : Vi, Galio").
   - `RoundPanel` says "suit sa wave" for a laner following its wave, instead of "reste en place".
5. **Flee toward the base**: every flee and every fight-loser retreat takes one step toward the own base from where the contact happened (`retreatStep`): lane middle → own T1, T1 → T2, T2 → T3, T3 → base. In the jungle and the river it goes to the neighbour closest to the base, as `retreatStep` already does.
   - A tied fight still sends both sides back to their previous position *(call: unchanged; the user spoke about fleeing)*.
6. **Objective offered from the spawn round**:
   - The "Faire …" prompt treats an objective spawning by the end of the round as up.
   - It is offered both to an ally standing on the pit and to an ally whose pending move ends on the pit with a step left.
   - The map label of a pit whose objective spawns this round says it is up ("Dragon en vie").
7. **Recall after the move** *(user's rule, read as: a recall can follow any move within the budget and always happens where the move ends)*:
   - The recall no longer needs a step left. A 2-step move then Recall recalls from the arrival.
   - In the UI, Recall keeps the pending move ("Déplacement puis recall"), and choosing a destination while a recall is pending keeps the recall.
   - A recaller whose arrival falls in a contact and flees recalls from where it fled to *(call)*. A fight still cancels the recall (unchanged).
8. **Join a fight 1 step away**:
   - When a prompted contact (fight or flee, not standard laning) happens, each champion of either side gets the choice "Rejoindre le combat" / "Rester" (default "Rester"). This applies to champions that:
     - are alive;
     - are not in any contact;
     - are not recalling;
     - arrived 1 step from any of the contact's positions.
   - A joiner moves 1 step into the contact, fights with its side, and forfeits its action this round *(call)*. It becomes visible to the other side, like any champion in a contact.
   - The AI joins when its side then wins a fight it would otherwise lose or tie *(call)*. The plan does not otherwise make the AI more aggressive.
   - Standard laning contacts (automatic) offer no join this playtest *(call: avoids a second prompt round; a jungler wanting to gank moves into the lane as today)*.
   - Joiners are decided before the contact's own choices resolve: the prompt shows the contact with its possible joiners, and both sides answer together.
10. **Passing over a ward reveals** *(call on the detail)*:
    - An enemy whose route this round crosses an own active ward is visible to that side for the round: position, level and HP, like a champion seen at the end of the round. Its arrival is shown where it ends if that position is seen; otherwise the ward position, as today's spotting does.
    - Today a ward passage only sets a "spotted" last-seen with no stats, so this must be checked and fixed.
    - Applies to every ward, the Scuttle ward included.
11. **Dewarding reveals**: an enemy who clears one of our wards is visible to us in that round, at the ward's position (or where it ends, if seen), with level and HP. The log says who cleared it.
9. **Objective progress**: a pit with a capture in progress, own or seen, shows on its map label who is taking it and the rounds left ("Dragon — Bleu, 1 round restant"). The enemy's capture shows only when it is visible, as today.
