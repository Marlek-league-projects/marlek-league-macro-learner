## Context and decisions (playtest 8 → 9)

Status: approved by the user on 2026-10-28; executed subagent-driven.

This plan comes from the playtest of `feat/mvp-and-playtest` at `267f2a6`.

**Playtest feedback** (the user's words, translated):
1. When fleeing, we should be able to say where we flee to.
2. The jungler was placed on a camp in round 0 and did not clear it after my round-1 move.
3. In a fight, the champions should lose HP.
4. The Scuttle does not place a ward.
5. The Scuttle must be taken at the same time as the objective next to it (Dragon or the Nashor pit).
6. XP does not go up much compared to the real game. Not doing your camps to gank must be a real tempo and XP sacrifice.

**Diagnosis** (engine reproduced with a script on seed 1):
1. Flee: `round.ts` `retreat` always takes `retreatStep`, one step toward the own base. Nothing in `ContactChoice` carries a destination.
2. Camp: by the rules, a jungler staying on its camp clears it, and 1 step away it clears the arrival camp. 2 steps away it arrives with no action and clears nothing, and the start camp stays up. The user moved away and expected the start camp done.
3. HP: in a decided fight only the surviving losers lose HP (`fight.survivorLoss` 15). Winners lose nothing; a tied fight (`stay`) costs nobody anything. Trades already cost both sides (20 / 5).
4. Scuttle ward: the engine places it (`takeScuttle`) and it reaches the view for 2 rounds (checked: placed round 6, in `viewFor` at rounds 7 and 8). Likely cause, to confirm in the browser: `MapView` draws the wards' `Eye` before the pawns, so the pawn standing on the Scuttle and its HUD cover the eye.

**Decisions** (user's unless marked *(call)*):
1. **Flee destination**:
   - After choosing "Fuir" in a prompted contact, the player may click, per fleeing champion, one position 1 step away. Without a click, today's automatic retreat (`retreatStep`) applies.
   - Allowed steps *(call)*: the neighbours of the spot the champion retreats from (as `retreat` computes it today). Excluded: the contact's positions, the enemy base, and every position an enemy of this contact came from this round (its `previous`). The automatic step is always among them, or the champion stays when it has none (as today).
   - Contract *(call)*: `ContactChoice` gets `fleeTo?: Record<string, string>` (champion id → position). The engine validates it and throws on a step not allowed. Helper `fleeSteps(state, data, contact, championId)` exported for the UI and the AI.
   - Unchanged: fleeing is still no action, the HP cost of fleeing is unchanged, and the flight creates no new contact.
   - The AI keeps the automatic retreat *(call)*.
   - Fog: the allowed steps only use what the prompt already shows (the contact's champions and the map).
2. **Start camp in round 1**:
   - A champion placed on a camp in round 0 clears it in round 1, wherever it moves. This is its round-1 action: it then has no action on arrival (no second camp, no farm, no objective) *(call)*.
   - Order *(call)*: the start camp is cleared at the start of round 1, before moves. An enemy arriving there finds it cleared.
   - Any role, not only the jungler *(call)*: a laner placed on a camp clears it and gives up its round-1 farm. Junglers first when two champions share a camp (as `clearCamps`).
   - A camp not up by round 1's end (none today: Krugs and Gromp spawn at 1:07, inside round 1) is not cleared.
   - UI: in round 1, a champion standing on its start camp shows "Fait le camp de départ, puis se déplace (sans action à l'arrivée)" when its order leaves the camp.
3. **HP in fights**:
   - Decided fight: the winners who survive lose `fight.winnerLoss` = 5 *(call: same as a won trade)*. The losers keep `survivorLoss` 15.
   - Tied fight: everyone in it loses `fight.tieLoss` = 10 *(call)*.
   - In a gank, the ganked side keeps `gankHpLoss` instead (never both, as today). The gankers winning pay `winnerLoss`.
   - Unchanged: objective race (no fight), farm under tower (`pressureHpLoss`), flee costs, trades.
   - A champion that lost HP does not regenerate that round (already the rule).
   - Benchmark: re-tune data only if it fails.
4. **Scuttle ward visible**:
   - Confirm the cause in the browser first: take the Scuttle with the jungler and look at the map the next round.
   - Fix *(call)*: draw the ward eyes above the pawns, and keep the eye clear of the pawn's HUD (offset). The eye's tooltip stays "Ward du Carapateur".
   - The round summary says "Ward du Carapateur posé" when a side takes the Scuttle *(call)*, if it does not already.
5. **Scuttle taken with the pit objective** (feedback added during the playtest: "the Scuttle must be taken at the same time as the objective next to it, Dragon or the Nashor pit"):
   - Already the rule (`captureObjectives`). Checked on seed 1: Dragon taken in round 14 also takes `scuttle-bot` and places its ward; the voidgrubs in round 23 also take `scuttle-top`.
   - Conditions today: the Scuttle is up, and no enemy is in the river (the pit or the Scuttle). The Scuttle is taken when the objective is *taken* (end of the 2nd round), not when it starts.
   - Browser check in Task 4: the log line "Bleu prend le Carapateur" and the eye after a Dragon.
6. **XP calibrated on the real game** (feedback: "XP does not go up much compared to the real game"):
   - Measured (scratch script, 10 seeds, Challenger AI vs Challenger AI), average level by time:

     | Role | 3:00 | 6:00 | 10:00 | 15:00 | 20:00 | 25:00 |
     |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
     | Top / Mid | 3.5 | 5.7 | 7.3 | 9.3 | 10.7 | 11.9 |
     | Jungle | 2.9 | 4.1 | 5.3 | 6.8 | 8.1 | 9.2 |
     | ADC / Support | 3.0 | 4.3 | 6.0 | 7.5 | 8.7 | 9.5 |

   - Targets *(call: orders of magnitude of a real game, from memory, not a checked source; tolerance ±1 level)*:

     | Role | 3:00 | 6:00 | 10:00 | 15:00 | 20:00 | 25:00 |
     |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
     | Top / Mid | 4 | 7 | 10 | 12 | 14 | 16 |
     | Jungle | 4 | 6 | 8.5 | 11 | 13 | 15 |
     | ADC | 3 | 5.5 | 8.5 | 11 | 13 | 15 |
     | Support | 3 | 5 | 7.5 | 10 | 12 | 13.5 |

   - Cause: every XP source is flat (600 XP/min of wave, 170 / 230 per camp, 250 per kill). In the real game they grow: waves (stronger minions, cannons), camps (their level follows the champions' average level), kills (the victim's level).
   - Mechanisms *(call on the shape; values tuned to the targets)*:
     - Wave XP per minute by stage (`economy.farmXpPerMin` becomes an array by stage).
     - Camp XP and gold grow with the average champion level when cleared: base × (1 + `camps.levelScaling` × (average level − 1)) (decision 7).
     - Kill XP grows with the victim's level (`economy.killXpPerLevel`).
   - A calibration script `packages/engine/scripts/xp-curve.ts` (`bun run xp-curve`) prints the measured table, so the targets can be re-checked later.
   - If the AI jungler does not clear enough to reach the jungle targets (level 2.9 at 3:00 today, where a full clear gives level 4), its pathing is in scope too.
7. **Skipping camps to gank is a real sacrifice** (feedback: "not doing your camps to gank must be a real tempo and XP sacrifice"):
   - Camps scale with the average level (decision 6): every round without a camp costs more as the game goes on.
   - A failed gank gives no reward: a jungler standing in a lane takes no wave farm (gold or XP), and the laner's share is unchanged *(call)*. Only a kill (or assist) pays, plus the lane effect of a won gank (wave to the tower, or a plate) as today.
   - The benchmark and the measured table are re-run after this change.
