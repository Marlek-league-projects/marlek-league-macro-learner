## Rules added (2f3417b)

- **Push stance**: the wave moves one extra step toward the enemy tower and hits it harder. The laner stands forward and is more exposed to ganks. In a lane contact Push behaves like Farm (no all-in).
- **Graded deaths**: the score gap sets how many losers die (`fight.deathGap` per death), most wounded first. Every losing survivor loses HP.
- **Ganks and lane position**: a pushed laner is exposed (bonus to the gankers), a laner in the middle is neutral, a laner under its own tower is protected. A gank that kills nobody still costs HP.
- **Crossing your own jungle**: going from top-side camps to bot-side camps routes through the mid T1 (2 moves), not through the river.
- **Recall** can place or clear a ward on its 1-step move.
- **Screen**: after a round resolves, the banner shows **Round suivant** next to **Revoir le tour**. Ward markers show the rounds each ward has left.
